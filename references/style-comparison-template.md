## 8 Dimensions Reference

1. **节奏控制** (rhythm) — tension/release cycles, climax placement, transition length
2. **伏笔埋设** (foreshadowing) — density, interconnection, reveal timing, suspense maintenance
3. **人物塑造** (character) — show vs tell, action-defined vs exposition, voice distinctness
4. **环境描写** (setting) — sensory detail density, mood service, info-dump vs immersion
5. **对话质量** (dialogue) — subtext, brevity, character voice, function vs personality
6. **心理描写** (psychology) — restraint, reader inference vs explicit, panel frequency
7. **爽点设计** (satisfaction) — cognitive dominance, resource harvest, reversal quality
8. **文笔质感** (prose) — economy, originality, repetition, metaphor quality
